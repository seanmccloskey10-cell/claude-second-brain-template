# Power-Ups

> **TL;DR:** Power-ups are optional upgrades that make your second brain more capable. The core vault works perfectly without any of them — add a power-up when you're ready, not on day one. Each one has its own page here: what it does, when to add it, and how to switch it on. To turn one on, just say *"set up the voice power-up"* (or whichever you want), or ask your tutor to walk you through it.

## What a power-up is

The core vault — capturing thoughts, building your wiki, weekly briefings — runs on your existing Claude plan with zero setup beyond the first-run wizard. **That's deliberate. Start there.**

Power-ups are the next layer. None of them are required. Each adds one capability, and each is something you switch on once you've felt where the plain vault leaves you wanting more. Add them one at a time.

## The menu

| Power-up | What it gives you | When to add it |
|---|---|---|
| **[Brain follows you everywhere](brain-follows-you.md)** | Claude knows your vault from *any* folder on your computer, not just the vault folder | **Early — this is the big one.** As soon as setup is done. It's what turns "a folder I open" into "an assistant that's always there." |
| **[Web Clipper](web-clipper.md)** | One-click capture of YouTube videos, X posts, and articles straight into your vault | Once you've got the daily rhythm and want reading-time to feed your brain automatically |
| **[Voice briefings](voice-briefings.md)** | Your weekly briefing as an MP3 you can listen to on a walk | When you're enjoying the written briefings and want them in your ears. Needs a free ElevenLabs key. |
| **[Ikigai](ikigai.md)** | A deep, vault-grounded self-interview that maps what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you're paid for | **Future-mode.** Only once you've got a few months of real content. Run it too early and you get aspirational fluff. |

## How to turn one on

You don't memorise anything. Open the page for the power-up you want and either:

- **Ask Claude to do it** — *"set up the voice briefings power-up"*, *"make my brain follow me everywhere"*, *"help me install the Web Clipper"*. Claude reads the matching page in this folder and walks you through it.
- **Or follow the page yourself** — each one is written so a non-technical person can do it step by step.

If your tutor set you up, they'll walk you through the first one or two on a screen-share. The rest you can add whenever you feel ready.

## The order most people add them

1. **Brain follows you everywhere** — right after setup. Biggest single upgrade.
2. **Web Clipper** — once capture-as-you-read sounds appealing.
3. **Voice briefings** — when you want the weekly briefing on a walk.
4. **Ikigai** — months in, when there's enough of you in the vault to reflect on.

There's no rush and no wrong order. The brain works fully without any of these.
